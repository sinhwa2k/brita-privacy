Brita Manager Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-24

Brita Manager (package com.sinhwa2k.brita, the "App") is an independently developed app for tracking the usage of a Brita water filter. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Information we collect

Information collected by the developer: none. The App has no server of its own, no sign-up or login, no payments, and does not use location, contacts, photos or similar permissions.

Information stored only on your device (never sent to the developer or any third party)

  • Settings you enter, such as filter capacity and the amount per pour
  • Filter usage and the reset date

All of it is removed when you uninstall the App or use "Settings > Apps > Brita Manager > Storage > Clear data".

Information collected by the ad SDK (Google AdMob)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads. The SDK may automatically collect and share the following.

  • IP address (may be used to estimate approximate location)
  • App interactions (launches, taps, etc.)
  • Diagnostics (app and SDK performance, launch time, unresponsive rate, energy usage, etc.)
  • Device or account identifiers (Android advertising ID, app set ID, etc.)

See Google's disclosure, the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data when you use partner sites or apps.

2. Purposes

  • Serving ads and measuring ad performance
  • Fraud prevention, such as invalid-click detection, and service security
  • App and SDK performance analysis

The developer does not access this information and uses no analytics or crash-reporting tools of their own.

3. Sharing with third parties

Ad-related information is sent to the ad provider, Google LLC (AdMob). It is not shared with any other third party. Transmission is encrypted with TLS.

4. Retention and deletion

  • In-app data: stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.
  • Ad data: retained under Google's policies. The developer cannot delete it, but you can limit or delete its use as described in section 5.
  • Send deletion or access requests to the contact below.

5. Opting out of personalized ads

  • Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads): "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • Google Ads Settings: https://adssettings.google.com

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn such information was collected, we delete it promptly.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, this document is updated and the effective date revised.
